Chain Crush Support

Need help with Chain Crush? Email sardorurdushev@gmail.com and tell us your phone model and what happened. We aim to reply within a few days.

How do I play?

Tap the right side of the screen to turn right and the left side to turn left. Your car drives itself. Drift in wide curves to swing the weapon on your chain into the police. Level 1 teaches you the rest.

Why do police cars hurt me when I hit them?

Police cars have spikes on the front. Hit them from the side or back, or swing your weapon into them.

Why does an enemy take more than one hit?

Bigger enemies are tougher. Vans, SUVs, trucks and tanks need several hits, and their health bar shows how many remain.

Why is there no sound?

Turn up your phone's volume and check that Sound Effects and Music are on in the game's Settings.

The Watch Ad button doesn't work. What can I do?

Ads need an internet connection and aren't always available. Check your connection and try again in a moment.

The game runs slowly. What can I do?

Turn off High Graphics in Settings, and close other apps.

Can I get my progress back?

Progress is saved only on your phone, so it can't be restored after you delete the game or use Reset Progress.

How do I change my ad choices?
